This is a model, not statistics

The deposits, the withdrawals and the crowd in the wave are simulated. An hour in the scene is squeezed into 12 seconds. The amounts 10 · 20 · 100 · 500 · 1,000 are an example of Available amounts. On this timeline waves leave at 13:00, 14:00 and 15:00; in the real form, “Next wave in” always shows when the next one leaves.

Here a withdrawal links to a deposit by three details: address, amount and time. In real life there are more. For example, an exchange knows whose account was topped up after a withdrawal. Don’t top up the withdrawal address from your main wallet. And don’t withdraw right after you deposit: the time blurs more when several hours pass between the deposit and the withdrawal.

Only withdrawals go out with a wave. Deposits and swaps don’t wait for one.

On a public blockchain

Every transfer is public

Salary, savings, paying a supplier: on a public blockchain anyone can see every transfer, forever. Deposits are on top, withdrawals below. Address, amount and time can link the two — each match is a red thread. Without the rules all three match: the withdrawal leads straight to the deposit.
